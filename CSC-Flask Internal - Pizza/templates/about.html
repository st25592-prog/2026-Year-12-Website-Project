About Dream Pizza

Welcome to Dream Pizza, your online destination for delicious pizzas and exciting flavours! We offer a wide variety of pizzas to suit different tastes, from classic cheesy favourites to unique and adventurous combinations. Our goal is to make ordering your favourite pizza easy, convenient and enjoyable.

Our Pizza Flavours

At Dream Pizza, we believe there is a pizza for everyone. Our menu includes a range of flavours, whether you prefer cheesy, savoury, sweet or traditional options.

  • Meat Lovers: A tasty choice for those who enjoy rich, meaty flavours.
  • Margherita: A classic favourite with simple, delicious flavours.
  • Hawaiian Style: A sweet and savoury combination.
  • Marinara: A traditional option for lovers of classic pizza flavours.
  • Seafood Style: A flavourful choice for seafood lovers.
  • Cheesy Garlic: A delicious combination of cheese and garlic.
  • Cheesy Pizza: A great option for anyone who loves cheesy goodness.
  • Beef & Onion: A savoury mix of beef and onion.
  • Caramel Glazed: A unique sweet-inspired flavour.
  • Greek-style Pizza: A Mediterranean-inspired choice.
  • Carbonara: Inspired by the rich, creamy flavours of carbonara.
  • Fried Pizza: A different and exciting take on a pizza favourite.

Make It Your Own

You can customise your order with our selection of add-ons, including mozzarella, Parmesan, garlic butter, hot-honey glaze and extra toppings. Mix and match your favourites to create a pizza experience that suits you.

Simple Online Ordering

With Dream Pizza, you can browse our menu, select your favourite pizzas, choose your quantities, add extra toppings and view your total before checkout. We want to make enjoying your favourite pizza flavours simple and convenient.
